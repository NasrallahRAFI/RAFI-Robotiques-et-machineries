The Team Behind the Machine

Experience that begins on the factory floor.

Our multidisciplinary team unites veteran mechanical engineers, automation specialists, and skilled field technicians. Each member is selected for their hands-on industrial background — the kind that understands a machine through its engineering blueprints, precision components, and real-world production stresses.

Five Disciplines / One Operational Reality

How our engineering advances.

01

Design & Simulation

We start from exact dimensional measurements to model reality, analyzing kinematics and simulating structural loads prior to cutting steel. Autodesk Inventor is used to model complete assemblies, executing finite-element stress and pressure analyses to validate component robustness. Iterations continue until the design is flawless.

02

Fabrication & Machining

Our heavy machining and metal fabrication heritage spans since 2008: CNC bending, shearing, roll bending, and punching on precision machines for clean, repeatable profiles. Abrasive waterjet cutting handles extra-thick plates. Machines are assembled in our workshop or directly on-site according to plant requirements.

03

Automation Programming

We program PLC systems on Siemens hardware, engineer custom HMIs, and integrate machines into factory industrial networks. Mastery of C#, C++, Python, G-code, and Ladder logic enables tailored control architectures that match the unique physical dynamics of each machine.

04

Heavy Mechanical Repair

Every overhaul begins with methodical physical diagnosis. Emergency interventions on robotic arms, CNC machining centers, and heavy welding lines are logged in detailed daily reports, from symptom isolation to final quality inspection under manufacturer tolerances.

05

Digitization & CNC Retrofit

Our portfolio spans retrofitted, modernized, or ground-up machine builds. Our proprietary Made in Morocco control unit provides tailored machine control that eliminates obsolescence and extends asset operating lifecycles by 10 to 15 years.
