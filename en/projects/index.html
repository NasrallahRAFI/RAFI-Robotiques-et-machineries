Armasteel
Custom DesignDelivered6 months

Two 5-axis welding robots — 16 meters of linear travel

Complete design, 3D modeling, fabrication, and programming of two 5-axis welding robots with real-time infrared tracking and 16 meters of linear travel for Armasteel.

01 / 05
01

The Industrial Challenge

Armasteel needed an automated system to make continuous, uniform welds on long steel profiles up to 16 meters, while maintaining consistency across different welding angles.

02

Deployed Engineering & Method

RAFI Robotiques et Machineries designed a 5-axis robotic system mounted on a 16-meter linear rail. Infrared sensors adjust the feed speed in real time to match the welding angle. We also developed a robust, easy-to-use operator interface.

03

Measurable Result

The two robots provide a 16-meter reach and produce high-quality welds without manual rework. The system increases productivity and can be used for heavy steel structures, shipbuilding, and automotive manufacturing.

Univers Acier
RepairDelivered12 Days

Custom bronze gear machining — Sharmaann boring mill

Complete disassembly, measurement and reverse engineering, and custom manufacture of special bronze gears for a Sharmaann horizontal boring mill at Univers Acier.

01 / 02
01

The Industrial Challenge

Univers Acier's heavy Sharmaann horizontal boring mill was out of service after its worm gears broke and became severely worn. The original parts were unavailable, and import delays were too long for the plant.

02

Deployed Engineering & Method

We disassembled the transmission in our workshop, measured and reverse-engineered the components, selected a wear-resistant bronze alloy, and manufactured the gears to order. We then reassembled the mechanism and checked clearances and tolerances.

03

Measurable Result

The machine was returned to service in 12 days. The transmission runs quietly without residual mechanical play, restoring boring accuracy and extending the machine's service life.

Belhou Frères
RepairDelivered10 Days

CNC Press Brake Recalibration and Shear Blade Adjustment

Complete geometric and electronic recalibration of a CNC press brake, with precision sharpening of the shear blades.

01 / 03
01

The Industrial Challenge

Belhou Frères' CNC press brake was out of service because its axis stops were misaligned and the shear blades were badly worn. The wear caused burrs and prevented clean cuts in thin sheet metal.

02

Deployed Engineering & Method

We diagnosed the equipment and recalibrated the press brake's mechanical and electronic components. We removed and ground the shear blades, sharpened them, and precisely adjusted the clearance between them.

Measurable Result

The equipment was returned to working order in 10 days. Bending angles were brought back to the required accuracy, and the shear produced clean cuts across the specified sheet thicknesses.
