03 Industrial Expertise

CNC Control Modernization & Retrofitting

Complete modernization of conventional machine tools into precise, long-lasting CNC machines.

CNC retrofitting extends the useful life of heavy machinery by replacing obsolete electronics with a modern numerical control system, without the cost of replacing the machine.

Our 2-, 3-, and 4-axis control cabinets include spindle drives, synchronous digital drives, high-dynamic servomotors, and precision-ground ball screws for backlash-free repeatability.

We provide our CNC controller, designed and manufactured in Morocco, with a 2-year warranty, technical support, and immediate local maintenance.
