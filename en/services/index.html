03 Industrial Expertise

CNC Digitization / Retrofitting

Total modernization of conventional machine tools into precise, high-durability CNC production units.

Retrofitting extends capital equipment ROI by replacing obsolete relays and analog drives with modern numerical control, without the heavy capex of acquiring new cast-iron machine beds.

Our engineered control enclosures for 2, 3, and 4 axes integrate heavy spindle drives, synchronized digital drivers, high-dynamic AC servo motors, and precision-ground ball screws for zero-backlash motion.

We deploy our proprietary CNC controller engineered in Morocco, backed by a comprehensive 2-year warranty and immediate local technical support.
